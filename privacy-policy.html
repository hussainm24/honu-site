Honu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Honu ("the app") is a habit, focus and reflection app for iPhone, offered by Mohammed Hussain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: your data stays on your phone and we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store or have access to any of your personal information. Honu has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

Information you enter, such as your habits and reasons, streaks, mood check-ins, journal entries, focus sessions and screen time entries, is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

No tracking, ads or analytics

Honu contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not have it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, Apple may include Honu's data in those backups under your own Apple account and settings. That is handled by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy. We don't have access to it.

Deleting your data

You can erase your data at any time by deleting the app from your phone (or using the reset option in Settings, if available). Because we don't hold a copy, nothing remains with us.

Children

Honu is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Honu is a self-improvement tool and not a medical service. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are struggling with your health or an addiction, please talk to a qualified professional.

Changes to this policy

If Honu ever changes how it handles data (for example, by adding optional accounts or sync), we will update this policy and describe the change in the app before it takes effect.
